  1. Pick a ? on the map. Each one is a lead on a real historic site in Birmingham.

The Martha Board

Explorers earn Marthas, named for Martha Baldwin: her name is on the library, and she rests in Greenwood Cemetery. Find sites, solve field tests, date your finds, excavate Layer 3 with today’s photos and recover relics to climb the board.

    Please be respectful. Most sites are private homes. Look from the sidewalk and don’t go onto the property.

    Sources. Histories and build years from the 2001 Birmingham virtual-tour files (City of Birmingham historic-district reports; Present Past in Birmingham, 1969, text by Billie Pierno and Judy Shumway) and the City of Birmingham’s designated historic districts list. Greenwood Cemetery: City of Birmingham and Michigan historical marker L1832. Photos: Present Past (1969), archival scans, and 2000–01 surveys by the virtual-tour volunteers.
